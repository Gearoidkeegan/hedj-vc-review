# Gap-analysis checklist

Diff the materials against this list. Anything absent, or present but empty of
substance, is a named gap rated **fatal / serious / cosmetic** for the company's stage.
A fatal gap is one no IC would proceed past without an answer.

## Core pitch elements (after Sequoia's business-plan template)

1. **Company purpose** — one declarative sentence. Missing = serious.
2. **Problem** — who hurts, how much, how often; how it's solved today.
3. **Solution** — why this, why it's 10x (not 10%) better; demo or concrete evidence.
4. **Why now** — what changed (technology, regulation, behaviour). Absence is the most
   common serious gap in otherwise polished decks.
5. **Market size** — bottom-up (price × addressable buyers), not "1% of $X bn".
   Top-down-only sizing = serious.
6. **Competition** — includes incumbents and "do nothing". A slide claiming "no
   competitors" = serious (usually signals weak market understanding).
7. **Product** — state today, roadmap, what the round ships.
8. **Business model** — who pays, how much, gross margin logic.
9. **Team** — founder–problem fit, relevant history, key hires needed.
10. **Financials** — actuals (if any), projections, burn, runway.
11. **The ask** — amount, instrument, valuation expectation, the milestone it buys.
    Ask with no milestone attached = serious.

## Stage-dependent expectations

- **Pre-seed:** MVP or credible progress evidence (Techstars: progress beats polish).
  No evidence of anything built = serious; missing cohort data = cosmetic.
- **Seed:** early usage/retention evidence; cohort or engagement data if retention is
  claimed (claimed-but-unshown = serious); financial model beyond one slide.
- **Series A+:** financial model with actuals vs plan; unit economics (CAC/LTV,
  payback); pipeline or cohort detail; missing any of these = fatal.

## Materials & presence expectations

- Financial model absent at seed+ = serious; at pre-seed = cosmetic.
- Investor memorandum / long-form plan absent at Series A+ = serious.
- No working website at seed+ = serious; dormant/abandoned site = red flag, not gap.
- No company LinkedIn or founder public presence at seed+ = serious (harms
  hiring and follow-on fundraising, and is itself a signal).
