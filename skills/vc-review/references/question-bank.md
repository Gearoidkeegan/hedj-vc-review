# Question bank

Seed questions for Stage 3 consolidation. Use only those the materials don't already
answer; merge with stage-output `questions[]`; always cite claim IDs where relevant.

## Thiel's seven (adversarial probes)

1. **Engineering** — is this a 10x breakthrough or a marginal improvement?
2. **Timing** — why is now the right time; what breaks if you're two years early?
3. **Monopoly** — do you start with a large share of a small market? Which one?
4. **People** — why is this the right team; what's the insider advantage?
5. **Distribution** — how do you reach customers, and what does a sale cost?
6. **Durability** — what does year 10 look like; what erodes the position?
7. **Secret** — what do you know that others don't (and why is it true)?

## Techstars-style screens

- Would you back this team with a different idea? Why / why not?
- What have you learned from users in the last 30 days?
- What has the team shipped together before this?
- What's the founders' unfair advantage in *this* market?

## IC classics

- What's the single assumption that, if wrong, kills the business? (pre-mortem link)
- Walk me through the bottom-up market size. (vs any top-down claim)
- Who loses if you win, and what will they do about it?
- Why hasn't an incumbent done this — and what happens when they try?
- What does the next round need to believe? Which milestones prove it?
- Where does the model break: churn +50%, CAC ×2, sales cycle ×2?
- Who on the cap table would a Series A investor object to?
- What did your last three lost deals/users say?

## Founder-facing (from background/digital tracks)

- Clarify the gap between <claimed role> and the public record. (only where the
  founder-check found an unresolved contradiction — cite it)
- The public story on <channel> says X, the deck says Y — which is current?
